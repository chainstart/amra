# Complete Wilson scan at order \(3^8=6561\)

Date: 2026-07-30

## Result

Every one of the \(1{,}396{,}077\) isomorphism types of groups of order
\(3^8\) in the SglPPow 2.4 catalogue was checked.  None is a counterexample
to Wilson's question

\[
 P_3(G)=\{g^3:g\in G\}\leq G
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 P_3(G)\text{ is powerful}.
\]

Together with the earlier complete scans at smaller powers of three, this
gives the computer-assisted lower bound

\[
 \boxed{\text{a \(3\)-group counterexample, if one exists, has order at
 least \(3^9=19683\).}}
\]

This is a finite-catalogue theorem.  It neither settles Wilson's question for
all \(3\)-groups nor addresses odd primes other than \(3\).

## Why the filter is logically complete

For each catalogue group \(G\), put

\[
 H=\operatorname{Agemo}(G,3)=\langle g^3:g\in G\rangle.
\]

The following implications justify every short circuit in the scan.

1. If the raw cube set \(P_3(G)\) is a subgroup, then it equals \(H\), because
   \(H\) is by definition the subgroup generated by that set.
2. Every abelian finite \(3\)-group is powerful.  Hence an abelian \(H\)
   cannot be a Wilson counterexample.
3. GAP's `IsPowerfulPGroup(H)` tests the target property directly.  Only when
   it is false is raw cube enumeration needed.
4. Since \(P_3(G)\subseteq H\) and \(\langle P_3(G)\rangle=H\), closure is
   equivalent to the single exact cardinality test
   \[
     |P_3(G)|=|H|.
   \]

Thus the scan does not assume exponent \(9\), rank two, a presentation family,
or a nilpotency-class cutoff.

The endpoints satisfy

\[
 [1,261686]\sqcup[261687,802107]\sqcup[802108,1396077]

so there is neither a gap nor an overlap.  The exact terminal rows and
resource measurements are frozen in `order6561_complete_wilson_scan.txt`.

## Reproduction

With SglPPow visible to GAP, a shard can be rerun by setting the two globals
and reading `validate_order6561_wilson_scan.g`.  The separate
`validate_order6561_nonpowerful_candidates.g` script is intentionally
independent of the shard counters and must also pass before quoting the
lower bound.

The static transcript is evidence, not an oracle: the two GAP scripts contain
the executable checks and abort on catalogue-size, candidate, powerfulness,
or closure discrepancies.
