# Status of the proposed sharp \(2p\) nilpotency barrier

Date: 2026-07-30

## Candidate statement

For an odd prime \(p\), consider
\[
 \exp(G)\mid p^2,\qquad \operatorname{cl}(G)<2p.                \tag{C}
\]
The proposed universal conclusion is
\[
 [x^p,y^p]=1\qquad(x,y\in G).                                  \tag{P}
\]

As of this audit, **(C) \(\Rightarrow\) (P) is not proved for arbitrary
groups**.  It must not be cited as a theorem.

## What is proved

- For arbitrary groups, (P) holds when
  \(\operatorname{cl}(G)\leq p\).
- For metabelian groups, (P) holds throughout the full proposed range
  \(\operatorname{cl}(G)\leq2p-1\); see
  `METABELIAN_2P_NILPOTENCY_BARRIER.md`.
- In \(\operatorname{UT}_{2p}(\mathbb F_p)\), all \(p\)-th powers commute.
- In \(\operatorname{UT}_{2p+1}(\mathbb F_p)\), an explicit pair of
  \(p\)-th powers does not commute.  Hence class \(2p\) is a genuine upper
  boundary and would be sharp if the universal candidate is proved.
- At \(p=3\), every exponent-nine SmallGroups type through order \(2187\)
  has abelian cube subgroup.  At order \(2187\), this covers 8,302
  exponent-nine groups, including 1,181 of class four and all 26 of class
  five.  The 26 class-five cases have their cube subgroup inside
  \(Z_3(G)\).

The order-\(2187\) computation is evidence at the endpoint
\(2p-1=5\), not a proof for all finite 3-groups.

## Why Hall divisibility alone stops short

In the Hall collection of \([x^p,y^p]\), every factor of total weight at
most \(p\) has coefficient divisible by \(p^2\).  Starting at weight
\(p+1\), bidegrees \((p,s)\) and \((r,p)\) can have coefficients divisible
by only one factor of \(p\).  The possible weights continue through
\(2p-1\).  At weight \(2p\), the bidegree \((p,p)\) coefficient can be a
unit, and the unitriangular example realizes it.

Thus a proof below class \(2p\) must use the exponent law
\(z^{p^2}=1\) to eliminate the one-\(p\) Hall terms.  Coefficient counting
without those induced relations is insufficient.

## Khukhro/Hughes boundary: relevant but not a refutation

Macdonald proved the Hughes conjecture for finite \(p\)-groups of class at
most \(2p-2\).  Khukhro produced conditional class-\(2p-1\),
exponent-\(p^2\) examples with Hughes subgroup of index \(p^2\), and the
relation-ideal condition is known for \(p=5,7,11\).

This makes class \(2p-1\) a high-risk endpoint.  It does **not** settle the
present power-commutator question:

- \(H_p(G)\) is generated by elements whose order is not \(p\);
- \(G^p\) is generated by \(p\)-th powers;
- the raw set \(P_p(G)=\{g^p:g\in G\}\) is a third object.

No implication from Khukhro's index calculation to
\([G^p,G^p]\ne1\) was found.  The construction therefore supplies a
priority test family, not a counterexample.

The later explicit Havas--Vaughan-Lee \(p=5\), class-nine anti-Hughes
family can be ruled out more strongly.  Their certified presentation output
has \(\exp(G')=5\), \(H_5(G)=\langle c\rangle G'\), and
\(c^5\in\gamma_9(G)\leq Z(G)\).  Hall collection then gives
\[
 P_5(G)\subseteq\langle c^5\rangle\gamma_6(G),
\]
an abelian subgroup because
\([\gamma_6(G),\gamma_6(G)]\leq\gamma_{12}(G)=1\).
See `HUGHES_CLASS_2P_MINUS1_POWER_AUDIT.md`.  Hence this most explicit
known class-\((2p-1)\) test family supports, rather than refutes, the
power-commutator candidate.

## Directed closure search at the \(p=3\), class-six boundary

The two length-three strings in
\(\operatorname{UT}_7(\mathbb F_3)\) generate a group of order \(3^{13}\),
class six, and exponent nine.  Its 649 raw cubes generate a nonabelian,
non-powerful group of order \(3^{10}\), but the raw set is not closed.
This near miss cannot be repaired inside the same section lattice:

- its centre is the order-three derived subgroup of the cube-generated
  group, so every nontrivial normal quotient kills all cube
  noncommutation;
- the cube-generated subgroup in each of its four maximal subgroups is
  abelian, excluding every proper subgroup and its quotients.

The associated 15-dimensional two-string algebra is equally exhausted:
every ideal quotient either kills \(A^3B^3\), making cubes commute, or
retains \(A^3B^3\), in which case
\((1+A^3)(1+B^3)\) is not a cube.  Separately, any exponent-nine
algebra-group counterexample must have algebra dimension at least nine by
human filtration and cube-power lemmas.  The exact seven-profile,
34-variable dimension-eight audit remains as an independent certificate.
All 29 dimension-nine filtration profiles now have human exclusions.  In
the last profile \((2,2,2,1,1,1)\), associativity across
one-dimensional \(A_4,A_5,A_6\) forces the sixfold multiplication tensor
to be a symmetric pure power, so cubes commute without a closure
assumption.  Hence the algebra bound \(\dim J\ge10\) is human-checkable.
The dimension-ten profile audit strengthens this to
\(\dim J\ge11\); the dimension-eleven sharp witness has noncommuting
cubes but fails raw-cube closure.  See
`UT7_TWO_STRING_SUBQUOTIENT_NO_GO.md` and
`ALGEBRA_GROUP_P3_BARRIERS.md`; the new hand proof is in
`DIM9_HUMAN_CUBE_COMMUTATIVITY.md` and
`DIM10_ALGEBRA_PROFILE_STATUS.md`.

These are closure/powerfulness exclusions at class six.  They neither prove
nor refute the unrestricted commuting-cubes candidate below class six.

## Precise remaining routes

1. Prove or refute the free-word inclusion
   \[
     [x^p,y^p]\in F^{p^2}\gamma_{2p}(F).
   \]
2. Compute the image of \([x^p,y^p]\) in Khukhro's original
   class-\(2p-1\) quotient.  The Havas--Vaughan-Lee \(p=5\) quotient is
   already excluded by its certified structural properties.
3. Extend the metabelian module argument through the derived series.  It
   already gives \([G^p,G^p]\leq G''\); the issue is to force the residual
   element from \(G''\) to weight at least \(2p\).
4. For \(p=3\), build the relatively free exponent-nine class-five
   quotient rather than relying only on bounded SmallGroups catalogues.
   At class six, raw-cube closure must now be used essentially:
   all dimension-ten filtered profiles have commuting cubes, whereas
   `DIM11_SHARP_NONCOMMUTING_CUBE_WITNESS.md` gives a dimension-eleven
   algebra with noncommuting cubes whose raw cube set is explicitly not
   closed.  The closure-aware dimension-eleven reduction excludes that
   length-six profile; a quadratic-relation lemma excludes
   \((2,2,3,1,1,1,1)\).  The last profile
   \((2,2,2,2,1,1,1)\) splits by leading cube dimension: the pure
   degree-seven tensor excludes the one-dimensional branch, while the
   quadratic short-chain lemma contradicts the cube bijection in the
   two-dimensional branch.  Thus the closure-aware algebra bound is
   \(\dim J\ge12\); see `DIM11_CLOSURE_AWARE_STATUS.md`.

Until one of these closes, the correct research label is “sharp candidate
with a proved metabelian theorem and strong \(p=3\) evidence.”
