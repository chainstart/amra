# Power audit of a class-\((2p-1)\) anti-Hughes family

Date: 2026-07-30

## A general exclusion lemma

Let \(p\) be an odd prime and let \(G\) be a finite \(p\)-group satisfying

\[
\operatorname{cl}(G)\leq2p-1,\qquad
\exp(G')\mid p.                                             \tag{1}
\]
Suppose there is an element \(c\in G\) such that

\[
 g^p=1\quad\text{for every }g\notin\langle c\rangle G',
 \qquad c^p\in Z(G).                                        \tag{2}
\]
Then every two raw \(p\)-th powers in \(G\) commute.

### Proof

Only an element of \(\langle c\rangle G'\) can have a nontrivial
\(p\)-th power.  Write such an element as \(g=c^k d\), where \(d\in G'\).
Collect \((c^k d)^p\).

The term \(d^p\) is trivial by (1).  Every collected commutator contains
\(d\), hence belongs to \(G'\).  Terms of collection degree less than \(p\)
have coefficients divisible by \(p\), so they also vanish because
\(\exp(G')\mid p\).  A term of collection degree at least \(p\) which
contains \(d\) has lower-central weight at least
\[
 (p-1)\cdot1+1\cdot2=p+1,
\]
because \(c\) has weight one and \(d\in G'\) has weight at least two.
Therefore
\[
  g^p\in \langle c^p\rangle\gamma_{p+1}(G).                  \tag{3}
\]
The first factor in (3) is central by (2), while
\[
 [\gamma_{p+1}(G),\gamma_{p+1}(G)]
 \leq\gamma_{2p+2}(G)=1
\]
by the class bound.  Thus the right side of (3) is abelian, and all raw
\(p\)-th powers commute.

If \(c^p\) has order dividing \(p\), (3) also shows that
\(\exp(G)\mid p^2\); no separate global exponent assumption is needed.

The lemma concerns pairwise commutativity.  It does not say that the raw
power-value set is closed under multiplication.

## Application to the Havas--Vaughan-Lee \(p=5\) construction

Havas and Vaughan-Lee provide Magma source and authenticated output for a
three-generator anti-Hughes 5-group of class nine.  Their output records:

```text
G is generated by a, b and c; and c has order 25
gamma_2(G) has class at most 4, so gamma_2(G) has exponent 5
So the Hughes subgroup is <c>G' and c^5 generates gamma_9(H)
```

After central-complement quotients, their construction reaches order
\(5^{54}\) while retaining \(c^5\ne1\); the final center has order five and
is generated by \(c^5\).

These certified properties match the lemma:

- the constructed group has class nine and \(c\) has order 25;
- \(G'\) has exponent five;
- every element outside the Hughes subgroup
  \(H_5(G)=\langle c\rangle G'\) has order five;
- \(c^5\in\gamma_9(G)\leq Z(G)\).

Consequently
\[
  P_5(G)\subseteq\langle c^5\rangle\gamma_6(G),
\]
and the right side is abelian.  Thus this explicit
class-\(2p-1\) anti-Hughes family has commuting fifth powers and is not a
counterexample to the proposed sharp-\(2p\) power-commutator barrier.

## Sources and reproducibility limit

Primary paper:

G. Havas and M. Vaughan-Lee,
[“On counterexamples to the Hughes
conjecture”](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2009.04.011),
*J. Algebra* **322** (2009), 791--801.

The authors' [supplementary Magma programs and
outputs](https://users.ox.ac.uk/~vlee/hughes/) include `p5g3mo.m` and
`p5g3mo.l`, from which the displayed structural facts are taken.

Magma is not installed in the present AMRA environment, so the original
power-commutator construction was not rerun locally.  The exclusion lemma
is independent of Magma; its application relies on the authors' published
program output.  A future audit should rerun `p5g3mo.m` under a current
Magma release and print the power subgroup explicitly.
