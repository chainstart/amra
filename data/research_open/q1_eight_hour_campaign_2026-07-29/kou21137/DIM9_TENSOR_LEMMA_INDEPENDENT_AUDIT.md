# Independent audit of the dimension-nine tensor proof

Audit date: 2026-07-30

## Verdict

The closure-free proof in `DIM9_HUMAN_CUBE_COMMUTATIVITY.md` for the last
profile
\[
(2,2,2,1,1,1)
\]
is valid.  Its conclusion uses only associativity, the power filtration,
\(J^7=0\), and
\[
\dim A_4=\dim A_5=\dim A_6=1,\qquad
A_i=J^i/J^{i+1}.
\]
It does not use the circle-product closure equation, a finite-field
enumeration, or the old SMT certificate.

## Checks performed

### 1. Surjectivity of the graded products

For the usual associative power filtration,
\[
J^iJ^j=J^{i+j}.
\]
Consequently the induced bilinear map
\[
A_i\otimes A_j\longrightarrow A_{i+j}
\]
is onto.  In particular, the left and right maps through each of the
one-dimensional layers used in the proof are nonzero.  This justifies the
nonzero linear forms in the tensor identities; they are not extra
genericity assumptions.

### 2. The multiplication tensors are nonzero

The graded algebra is generated by \(V=A_1\), because every element of
\(J^k/J^{k+1}\) is represented by a sum of products of \(k\) elements of
\(J/J^2\).  Hence the scalar word tensors
\[
f_i:V^{\otimes i}\longrightarrow A_i\cong\mathbb F
\quad(i=4,5,6)
\]
are nonzero.

### 3. The shift-tensor lemma

The proof uses
\[
0\ne T\in(V^*)^{\otimes m},\qquad
T\otimes r=\ell\otimes T,\qquad \ell,r\ne0.
\]
Taking the first-mode span shows that the first factor span of \(T\) is
\(\langle\ell\rangle\), so \(T=\ell\otimes T'\).  Cancelling the nonzero
first factor gives the same identity for \(T'\).  Iteration reaches the
two-factor identity and yields
\[
T=c\ell^{\otimes m},\qquad r\in\langle\ell\rangle.
\]
This argument is valid over \(\mathbb F_3\); it uses no division by a
field-dependent integer.

### 4. Associativity forces one common pure tensor

After bases of \(A_4,A_5,A_6\) are fixed, associativity gives
\[
f_5=f_4\otimes r=\ell\otimes f_4.
\]
The shift lemma makes \(f_4\) and \(f_5\) pure powers of \(\ell\).
Applying it once more to
\[
f_6=f_5\otimes s=t\otimes f_5
\]
shows that \(t\) is proportional to \(\ell\) and
\[
f_6=d\ell^{\otimes6}.
\]
The comparison of the two pure expressions for the same nonzero \(f_5\)
is legitimate: equality of two nonzero decomposable tensor powers forces
their one-dimensional factor spans to agree.

### 5. Passage from the graded identity to arbitrary lifts

For arbitrary \(a,b\in J\), let \(x=a+J^2\) and \(y=b+J^2\).  The image in
\(A_6\) of
\[
a^3b^3-b^3a^3\in J^6
\]
is
\[
f_6(x,x,x,y,y,y)-f_6(y,y,y,x,x,x)=0
\]
because \(f_6\) is symmetric.  Thus the commutator belongs to \(J^7\),
which is zero.  Higher-filtration components of \(a,b\) cannot contribute
before degree seven, so no homogeneity assumption on the lifts is hidden.
