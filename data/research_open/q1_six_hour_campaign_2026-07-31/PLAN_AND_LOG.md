## Success hierarchy

1. a complete solution of one frozen public-problem gate;
2. an unconditional all-parameter theorem or counterexample that changes
   the proof architecture;
3. a smaller exact universal gate with a proof-backed implication;
4. a falsification that permanently removes a plausible but false route.

Finite evidence alone is never ranked above level 4.
