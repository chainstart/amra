# Four-problem Q1 research campaign

Date: 2026-07-30
Wall-clock contract: eight hours of research effort, parallelized across four
independent proof lines.  The clock allocation below is a research budget, not
a claim that an open problem must yield within eight hours.

## Acceptance standard

The primary target is an unbounded theorem with a proof that can support a
top-quartile mathematics manuscript.  A finite computation, a fixed-rank
identity, a conditional reformulation, or a numerical pattern is evidence only.
Every line must therefore separate:

1. proved unconditional theorems;
2. conditional reductions and exact equivalences;
3. finite falsifier/regression evidence;
4. conjectural steps and failed routes.

A line is promoted to a paper nucleus only after an independent proof audit,
exact regression checks where relevant, and a nearest-neighbour literature
comparison.  Solving the named open problem is not asserted unless every
quantifier in its public statement is closed.

## Eight-hour allocation

| Window | Four parallel lines | Root review |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00--0:30 | Freeze theorem contracts, inherited lemmas, forbidden overclaims, and literature boundary | Check public problem statements and priority risks |
| 0:30--4:00 | First proof attack; derive exact structural lemmas and try the strongest unbounded route | Attack Erdős #776 and monitor all four lines |
| 4:00--4:30 | Stop, red-team, and report the first genuinely open inference | Kill finite-only routes and reallocate effort to the strongest two lines |
| 4:30--7:00 | Second proof attack on surviving routes; independent derivation or adversarial counterexample search | Cross-audit the strongest claims and run exact tests |
| 7:00--8:00 | Freeze theorem statements, ledgers, reproduction commands, and next obstructions | Publication triage and unified final report |

## Line contracts

### Erdős #1083

Primary goal: an unconditional improvement to the recognized
three-dimensional distinct-distance exponent.  Secondary publishable target:
an unbounded Euclidean structure theorem that eliminates a full concentration
branch in the current matching-or-hub reduction.  The immediate attack is the
moderately-rich reverse-circle sector: force two incidence-rich, non-aligned
circles or prove a sharp obstruction showing exactly why that forcing fails.
One-circle and coaxial/concentric calculations already ruled out in prior work
are not to be repeated as if they advanced the exponent.

### OPG-1757

Primary goal: all-parameter pairwise negative correlation, first on the
complete-split model and then under a graph operation broad enough to imply the
original conjecture.  The proof attacks, in order, are: full-domain positivity
of the ordinary symbol; real-rooted/interlacing or normalized Newton control of
the middle band; and a complete second-active-coefficient or Rayleigh-preserving
operation.  Fixed pages or fixed coefficient bands do not meet the primary
standard.

### Erdős #809

Primary goal: a global stability or minimal-counterexample reduction that
forces every extremal graph into the already proved near-complete-split branch,
thereby closing the original asymptotic statement.  Secondary target: an
unbounded stability theorem covering a quantitatively natural class strictly
larger than the inherited split model.  The first attack is the low-minimum-
degree / near-two-clique branch needed by the induction.

### Erdős #776

Primary goal: prove the all-parameter rank-eight entry inequality

\[
D_8<\binom{V-11}{8}\qquad(V\ge 40),
\]

for the shortened zero-seed Macaulay orbit

\[
D_{V-12}=0,\qquad D_{q-1}=V+\operatorname{KK}_q(D_q).
\]

Together with the inherited fixed-depth descent, this would close the current
five-term colex route and give \(n_0(r)\le 2r+5\).  The exact residual form

\[
D_8=\binom{V-12}{8}+\binom{V-13}{7}+W_6
\]

reduces the new work to \(W_6<\binom{V-13}{6}\).  The first attack seeks a
symbolic carry envelope or a self-similar residual recurrence; large finite
margins alone are explicitly non-proofs.

## Output contract

Each problem directory contains a final report, a claim ledger, proof notes,
and reproducible audit code/data where computation is used.  The root report
will rank outcomes as: named problem closed, manuscript-grade unbounded
theorem, useful structural lemma, exact reduction, or negative/no-go result.
