# Narrow literature and priority note

## Closest direct source

Matija Bucić, Kaizhe Chen, and Jie Ma,
*On a maximal anti-Ramsey conjecture of Burr, Erdős, Graham, and Sós*,
arXiv:2603.18952v1 (19 March 2026).

Their theorem proves, for every \(k\ge4\) and the full nontrivial edge
range,
\[
f(n,e,C_{2k+1})
=\frac e2+\frac n2\sqrt{e-\frac{n^2}{4}}+o(n^2).
\]
They explicitly state that \(k=3\) needs a more involved stability
argument in their second case and that their first case is the main
bottleneck.

## Strict increment in this package

Relative to the public BCM26 manuscript and the inherited R002--R004
artifacts, this package supplies:

1. a fully written near-Dirac \(C_7\) theorem with no split assumption;
2. a quantified exact-four-path obstruction dichotomy;
3. closure of the near-two-clique no-three-step branch;
4. a maximum-cut core/hub theorem that no longer assumes an independent
   side.

These claims are mathematical increments in the local proof chain.  This
note does not assert global priority.
