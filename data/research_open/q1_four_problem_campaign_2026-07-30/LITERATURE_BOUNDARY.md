# Nearest-neighbour literature boundary

Search date: 2026-07-30 (Asia/Hong_Kong).

The campaign used a multi-source, primary-source-first search.  Queries combined
the public problem numbers/titles with the current technical terms
`distinct distances R3`, `maximal anti-Ramsey C7`, `antichain multiplicity
n0(r)`, and `uniform spanning forest Rayleigh`.  arXiv records were deduplicated
by arXiv identifier/version and checked against the public problem pages.
This is a priority firewall, not a complete MathSciNet/zbMATH novelty opinion.

## Erdős #1083

- The current [Erdős Problems #1083 page](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1083)
  records the three-dimensional lower bound
  \(f_3(n)\gg n^{3/5}\) and asks whether the exponent can reach
  \(2/3-o(1)\).  Therefore a structural dichotomy or synchronization lemma is
  not described as an exponent improvement unless it actually yields a bound
  above \(3/5\).
- The campaign's reverse-circle work must also be compared at manuscript stage
  with the incidence/classification papers already cited on that page and in
  the inherited #1083 report.  The present search found no source announcing a
  post-\(3/5\) unconditional exponent.

## Erdős #809

- The current [Erdős Problems #809 page](https://www.erdosproblems.com/809)
  says that Bucić, Chen and Ma solved the conjecture for \(k\ge4\), while the
  full problem remains open.
- Bucić, Chen and Ma,
  [*On a maximal anti-Ramsey conjecture of Burr, Erdős, Graham, and
  Sós*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18952), prove the
  \(n^2/8+o(n^2)\) asymptotic for \(C_{2k+1}\) when \(k\ge4\).
  Consequently this campaign's genuine unresolved target is \(k=3\), i.e.
  \(C_7\); merely reproving their \(k\ge4\) range has no priority value.

## Erdős #776

- He and Tang,
  [*An Erdős--Trotter problem on antichains with multiplicity \(r\) on each
  occurring level*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09803), arXiv:2602.09803v2,
  prove
  \[
  2r+2\le n_0(r)\le
  2r+2\log_2 r+O(\log_2\log_2 r).
  \]
- Their Problem 5.1 asks whether the error beyond \(2r\) is bounded, and they
  report \(2r+5\) constructions for several small \(r\).  Thus an all-\(r\)
  proof of the inherited colex route \(n_0(r)\le2r+5\) would directly answer
  their stated next problem and is potentially a major result.  A finite scan
  or a conditional Macaulay gate does not answer it.

## OPG-1757

- Grimmett and Winkler,
  [*Negative association in uniform forests and connected
  graphs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0302185), formulate the uniform-forest
  negative-correlation conjecture and give finite computational verification.
- Fang and Ma,
  [*Gårding Polynomials*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27755), v2,
  prove Rayleigh consequences for several matroid/graph classes but retain as
  Conjecture 14.16 that the unrooted spanning-forest generating polynomial of
  every graph is Rayleigh.  They also identify the stronger graphic
  C-Gårding route as an open problem.
- Tang and Zhang,
  [*Pairwise Negative Correlation for Uniform Spanning Subgraphs of the
  Complete Graph*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10738), prove asymptotic
  pairwise negative correlation for specified uniform families on \(K_n\).
  This does not settle arbitrary host graphs.

Hence OPG-1757 remains a live arbitrary-graph problem, but any manuscript must
distinguish an ordinary complete-split coefficient theorem from Fang--Ma's
multivariate Gårding/Rayleigh boundary and from Tang--Zhang's complete-graph
asymptotics.
