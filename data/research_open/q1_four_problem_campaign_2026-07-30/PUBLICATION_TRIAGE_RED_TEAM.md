# Four-problem campaign — independent publication triage red team

Date: 2026-07-30

## 1. Audit scope and boundary

This audit reads the four final reports, claim ledgers, independent audits,
and the second-round attack files in
`q1_four_problem_campaign_2026-07-30`.  It evaluates the mathematical
result packages, not a finished submission: no unified manuscript,
complete bibliography, external expert report, or journal decision is
present.

The assessment uses five evidence tiers.

1. **Named problem closed.** Every quantifier in the public problem is
   discharged.
2. **Manuscript-grade unbounded theorem.** A meaningful theorem over an
   unbounded natural parameter range has a closed proof and can serve as the
   mathematical centre of a specialist manuscript.  This does not itself
   certify novelty, journal fit, or a Chinese Academy journal quartile.
3. **Useful structural theorem.** A rigorous unbounded or finite-real theorem
   that materially narrows the problem but is too local, conditional, or
   technically narrow to carry the paper alone.
4. **Exact reduction.** A proved equivalence, conditional gate, obstruction
   dichotomy, or no-go result that identifies the remaining inference.
5. **Finite evidence.** Exact scans, interpolation data without a degree
   theorem, falsifier checks, and regression tests.  These support proofs but
   do not establish an unbounded claim.

Journal quartiles are database-, year-, and category-dependent.  The phrases
“Q1 seed” and “Q1 route” below are comparative research judgments, not
placement guarantees.

## 3. Erdős #1083

### 3.1 Safe mathematical classification

**Named problem:** open.  No fixed \(\delta>0\) is proved in
\[
f_3(N)\gg N^{3/5+\delta},
\]
and the requested \(2/3-o(1)\) exponent is not reached.

**Useful structural theorem:** proved.  For distinct nonzero dilates,
\[
\sum_\lambda E^+(T_\lambda,\lambda X)
\le
|X|\sum_\lambda|T_\lambda|
+|T_\ast|(|T_\ast|-1)|X|(|X|-1)
\]
is an exact finite-real theorem.  Its aggregate-support and
two-spectrum-overlap consequences were independently reconstructed.

**Conditional endpoint theorem:** proved within its explicit upstream
critical-codegree setup.  A surviving \(2/9\) hub forces:

- pairwise nonaligned congruent rich circles;
- \(t^{35/18-o(1)}\) labels of retained row degree
  \(t^{13/18-o(1)}\);
- \(t^{17/6-o(1)}\) synchronized row pairs at every fixed
  \(t^{2/9-\varepsilon}\) overlap threshold.

These statements sharpen the inverse structure.  They do not contradict
the hub, because the proof supplies a lower bound on spectrum overlap while
the missing geometric theorem must limit or classify that overlap.

**Finite evidence:** exact rational systems, geometric formula records, and
the focused dependency suite are regression evidence only.  The written
energy proof, not the 56 instances, establishes the unbounded statement.

### 3.2 Publication readout

The result is of interest to discrete geometry, incidence geometry, and
additive-combinatorial energy methods.  Its main strengths are exact
quantifiers, a real all-parameter inequality, and a much sharper description
of the surviving hub.

It is not an independent main theorem at the desired publication level:

1. the recognized distinct-distance exponent is unchanged;
2. the main Euclidean statement is conditional on the upstream critical
   setup;
3. the synchronized exceptional network survives rather than being
   eliminated;
4. the rich plane-pair matching branch remains independent and open;
5. priority for the elementary multidilate inequality is not exhaustively
   cleared.

The safe role is **a substantial section and endpoint theorem in a larger
inverse-structure paper**, not a standalone resolution paper.

### 3.3 Unique minimum closure theorem

There is no honest hub-only lemma that closes #1083, because the matching
branch survives separately.  The minimum named-problem closure must therefore
be stated as one two-input endpoint theorem:

> **Critical \(2/9\) endpoint expansion theorem.** There is a fixed
> \(\eta>0\) such that every configuration in the audited critical
> matching-or-hub alternative determines at least \(t^{3+\eta}\) relevant
> distances: in the hub input, prove a global spectrum de-reuse bound outside
> explicitly classified affine-quadratic exceptions and discharge every
> exception; in the rich plane-pair matching input, prove a Euclidean
> expansion bound with the same fixed saving.

The currently proposed bundle de-reuse inequality closes only the hub half.
It must not be described as closing #1083 by itself.

## 4. OPG-1757

### 4.1 Safe mathematical classification

**Named problem:** open.  Neither arbitrary-host pairwise negative
correlation nor the complete-split Rayleigh theorem is proved.

**Useful unbounded theorem 1:** proved.  The kernel window
\[
[\beta^r]K_k(s,\beta)>0
\qquad(k,s\ge4,\ 0\le r\le8)
\]
is an all-parameter theorem.  The 746 exact values are a polynomial
identity certificate because bidegree bounds and complete interpolation
rectangles are proved.  They are not merely a parameter scan.

**Useful unbounded theorem 2:** proved conditional on the inherited analytic
zero theorem.  The dominant zero is bracketed by
\[
1.961<\rho<1.962,
\]
and the leading long-band sequence has an explicit geometric asymptotic with
relative error \(<63(327/350)^n\).  This is an all-rank asymptotic, but only
for the highest-depth coefficient sequence.

**Open bridges:** all kernel ranks, the alternating pooled inversion, the
middle Newton/real-rootedness band, the complete \(\alpha^2\) layer, and the
arbitrary-host graph operation.

**Finite evidence:** independent Cayley recurrence values and holdout points
are implementation audits.  They do not form a second all-parameter proof.

### 4.2 Publication readout

The results are technically clean and potentially valuable to probabilistic
combinatorics, spanning-forest enumeration, matroid negative dependence, and
analytic combinatorics.  The positive shifted formulas and dominant-zero law
are suitable manuscript sections.

They do not independently support the desired main-theorem positioning:

1. rank eight is a fixed coefficient-depth window;
2. the pooled alternating inversion is untouched;
3. the spectral theorem controls one leading layer rather than the full
   coefficient array;
4. complete-split Rayleigh is not reached;
5. arbitrary host graphs are further away still;
6. external priority comparison with Tang--Zhang, Fang--Ma, and older
   forest-enumeration work is incomplete.

The safe role is **two strong technical components of a larger
negative-dependence manuscript**.

### 4.3 Minimum closure statement

The current proof tree does not justify one local lemma whose proof alone
would solve the named arbitrary-graph OPG.  It has two logically independent
gaps:

1. an **all-depth pooled complete-split positivity theorem** that removes the
   alternating inversion and proves the full \(\alpha^2\)/Rayleigh
   difference; and
2. a **Rayleigh-preserving arbitrary-host transfer theorem** broad enough to
   pass from that model to every graph.

For the present manuscript nucleus, the unique next closure theorem is the
first:

> **All-depth pooled-kernel theorem.** For every admissible \(k,s\), all
> coefficients after the actual pooled inversion defining \(F_k\) and
> \(B_k\) are nonnegative, hence the complete \(\alpha^2\) Rayleigh layer
> holds without a fixed coefficient-rank cutoff.

This would create a plausible independent main theorem for the
complete-split family.  It would still not, without the second transfer
theorem, close OPG-1757.  Any document listing a single local coefficient
identity as the unique missing step to the named problem is overstating the
current reduction.

## 5. Erdős #809

### 5.1 Safe mathematical classification

**Named problem:** open.  The \(C_7\) Case-1 range with minimum degree
linearly below \(n/2\) is not controlled.

**Manuscript-grade unbounded theorem:** proved and independently audited:
\[
e(G)>\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor,\qquad
\delta(G)\ge n/2-o(n)
\Longrightarrow
\chi_{\mathrm{rainbow}\ C_7}(G)
\ge n^2/8-o(n^2).
\]

This is the strongest publication object in the campaign.  It combines:

- quantified exact-four-path obstruction stability;
- exhaustive near-two-clique / near-complete-bipartite structure in the
  near-Dirac range;
- dense-\(C_7\) compatibility in the two-clique branch;
- a maximum-cut core/hub theorem in the near-bipartite branch.

**Useful structural theorem:** failure of one exact four-edge path at
minimum degree \(N/2-o(N)\) forces
\[
O((N-2\delta+1)N)
\]
edit distance from two balanced cliques or a balanced complete bipartite
graph.

**Exact reduction:** two same-colour good edges are unified by the exact
vertex-disjoint \((2,3)\)-path-linkage criterion.  The full fixed-\(s\)
Case 1 reduces to controlling the total excess of the good-edge colour
classes.

**Finite evidence:** the obstruction profiles, \(C_7\) splices, core/hub
templates, and rectangle optimization all pass their guards.  These validate
identities and templates, not the asymptotic theorem.

### 5.2 Publication readout

This theorem will interest extremal graph theorists, anti-Ramsey
researchers, and researchers studying prescribed-path stability.  The proof
has a coherent global statement, an independently audited logical chain, and
a natural asymptotic scale.

It is nevertheless not yet safe as an independent Q1-level headline:

1. the named \(C_7\) problem remains open;
2. Bucić--Chen--Ma already announce an unspecified \(k=3\) Case-2 stability
   argument, creating a real novelty and priority risk;
3. the exact-four-path theorem has not been exhaustively compared with the
   prescribed-path stability literature;
4. part of the proof package imports the BCM induction interface and an
   inherited split theorem;
5. no external specialist proof report is available.

The conservative position is **a manuscript-grade specialist theorem,
Q2-candidate / Q1-seed**, not a promised journal placement.  It could become
a stronger independent paper by broadening the exact-path stability theorem
or by closing Case 1.

The phrase “BCM-style Case 2 is closed” must retain its condition: it closes
that branch inside BCM's all-\(e\) strong-induction framework.  It does not
bypass the induction hypothesis in Case 1.

### 5.3 Unique minimum closure lemma

The second attack identifies an exact sufficient and essentially minimal
statement.  Let \(E_{\rm good}\) be the BCM good edges and
\[
M_\gamma=
E_{\rm good}\cap\{\text{edges of colour }\gamma\}.
\]
Then the unique current closure target is:

> **Linkage-defect lemma.** Under the fixed-\(s\) Case-1 contract,
> \[
> \sum_\gamma(|M_\gamma|-1)_+=o(n^2).
> \]

The good-edge count is already
\[
\left(\frac18+\frac s2+\frac{s^2}{2}-o(1)\right)n^2,
\]
so this lemma gives the required colour lower bound and closes the remaining
BCM Case 1.  Every repeated edge can be rooted in exactly one of the two
proved obstruction types: a three-vertex cover of all outer length-three
paths or a zero-codegree outer pair.

The missing proof ingredient is bounded-congestion charging.  The linear
\(2sn\) and \(4sn\) separator/bad blocks can currently absorb
\(\Theta(n^2)\) charges.  Internal dense blocks alone cannot fix this:
even under favourable colour separation their guaranteed count falls below
the target once
\[
s>\frac{1-\sqrt{4/5}}2.
\]

## 6. Erdős #776

### 6.1 Safe mathematical classification

**Named problem:** open.  The construction \(n_0(r)\le2r+5\) is not proved
for all \(r\), and the bounded-error question of He--Tang is not answered.

**Useful structural theorem / exact gate:** for every \(V\ge175\),
\[
D_{16}\le
\binom{V-12}{16}+\binom{V-13}{15}+V-1
\Longrightarrow
D_8<\binom{V-11}{8}.
\]
The separated canonical descent and coefficient table are independently
audited.  This replaces a rank-eight degree-six residual condition by a
linear residual condition at rank 16.

**Additional structural theorem:** the diagonal-suspension inequality between
adjacent \(V\)-orbits is all-parameter, and its exact gap recursion reduces
the proposed Lipschitz bound to a rank-17 shadow-loss inequality.

**Conditional block theorem:** a uniform
\(O(\log\log V)\) bound for the actual moving-block entry rank would keep the
residual separated and imply \(W(V)=V^{1/2+o(1)}<V\).  The inherited
first-carry theorem does not prove this later-entry statement.

**Finite evidence:** the premise holds at selected large \(V\), the
rank-eight entry is verified for \(40\le V\le174\), and adjacent residual
jumps satisfy the candidate bound at sampled points.  None closes the
unbounded \(V\)-quantifier.

### 6.2 Publication readout

The line is potentially important to extremal set theory, Macaulay/Kruskal--
Katona dynamics, and antichains with prescribed level multiplicity.  If the
remaining residual inequality were proved, the resulting constant-error
bound would directly address He--Tang's stated bounded-error problem and
could plausibly be a high-impact specialist result.

The current package is not such a result.  It is an unusually precise
conditional reduction:

1. the all-\(V\) premise remains open;
2. selected residuals and large numerical margins are finite evidence;
3. the Lipschitz and entry-rank proposals are sufficient but stronger than
the actual gate;
4. the first-carry \(O(\log\log V)\) theorem cannot be silently transferred
to the later moving-block entry event.

The safe role is **a proof-engineering section and exact closure gate in a
future #776 paper**.

### 6.3 Unique minimum closure lemma

The minimum statement is the rank-16 residual bound itself, not the stronger
Lipschitz conjecture:

> **Rank-16 residual lemma.** For every \(V\ge175\),
> \[
> D^{[V]}_{16}
> \le
> \binom{V-12}{16}+\binom{V-13}{15}+V-1.
> \]

Equivalently, the separated residual
\[
W(V)=D^{[V]}_{16}
-\binom{V-12}{16}-\binom{V-13}{15}
\]
satisfies \(W(V)<V\).

Together with the proved linear gate, the inherited rank-eight-to-rank-two
descent, and the exact finite bridge \(40\le V\le174\), this closes the
current five-term colex route to \(n_0(r)\le2r+5\).

The adjacent-parameter Lipschitz inequality, rank-17 shadow-loss bound, and
moving-entry \(O(\log\log V)\) estimate are alternative sufficient methods
for proving this lemma.  They are not additional conclusions already
available.

## 7. Cross-problem publication strategy

### 7.1 What can be submitted as a main theorem now?

Only #809 currently has a mathematical result with the scale and coherence
of a standalone specialist paper.  Even there, submission should wait for:

- a full novelty audit, especially against BCM and prescribed-path
  stability;
- a self-contained statement of all imported lemmas;
- an external graph-theory proof audit;
- a positioning decision: broaden the stability theorem, or explicitly
  present it as a near-Dirac partial result.

The other three should not be split into thin standalone papers merely
because their auxiliary theorems are unbounded.

### 7.2 What outcomes would materially change the ranking?

1. **#776 rank-16 residual lemma:** would immediately promote #776 to a
   potential major specialist main theorem and answer a stated bounded-error
   direction.
2. **#809 linkage-defect lemma:** would close the missing \(C_7\) case in the
   BCM route and become the strongest publication result of this campaign.
3. **OPG all-depth pooled-kernel theorem:** would promote the complete-split
   line to a credible standalone negative-dependence theorem; an additional
   transfer remains necessary for the named arbitrary-graph conjecture.
4. **#1083 critical endpoint expansion theorem:** would create an exponent
   improvement only if both hub and matching inputs receive a fixed saving.

### 7.3 Coherence warning

The four topics are mathematically unrelated enough that combining their
partial results into one conventional research article would weaken the
scientific narrative.  They should remain separate proof programs.  The
campaign-level document is appropriate as an internal research report, not
as a single external manuscript.

## 8. Risk and unsupported-claim register

The following claims are not supported by the current evidence:

- any of the four named problems is solved;
- the #1083 synchronized hub alone improves the \(3/5\) exponent;
- eight OPG kernel ranks imply all-rank positivity, full Rayleigh, or
  arbitrary-host negative correlation;
- the #809 Case-2 theorem bypasses BCM Case 1;
- selected #776 residual values prove the all-\(V\) gate;
- any current result is guaranteed acceptance in a Chinese Academy Q1
  journal.

The following are supported but must be phrased narrowly:

- #1083 has a valid all-parameter energy theorem and a conditional endpoint
  synchronization theorem;
- OPG has two real unbounded auxiliary theorems despite one having fixed
  coefficient depth;
- #809 has a complete near-Dirac unbounded theorem and is the strongest
  current paper nucleus;
- #776 has an audited all-parameter implication and an exact unbounded
  closure target.

This ordering measures present publication readiness, not ultimate
mathematical importance.
