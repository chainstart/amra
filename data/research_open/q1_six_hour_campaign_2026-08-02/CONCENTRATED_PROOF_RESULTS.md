Let \(v\) be a maximum-degree witness, put
\(g=\Delta(G)-\delta(G)\), and suppose \(b,c\notin N[v]\) obey

\[
bc\notin E(G),\qquad
N(b)\cap N(c)=\varnothing,\qquad
E(N(b),N(c))=\varnothing.
\]

Pure missing-pair accounting gives the parity-sharp bounds

\[
n\le
\begin{cases}
2g^2-2g-6,&n\ \mathrm{even},\\
2g^2-2g-3,&n\ \mathrm{odd}.
\end{cases}
\]

The proof is pairwise: one leaf has
\(\ell=t=1,r=\rho,d=\rho-1\), and concavity eliminates the centre
degree from the maximum-degree deficit budget.  No reserve or colouring
estimate is used.  Explicit cyclic two-clique graphs attain both constants;
for every \(g\ge5\) they satisfy \(L_4(2)\), and injective colouring is
rainbow on every \(C_7\).

The sharpness graphs can be recoloured to realize a zero-shore pair of
multiplicity \(g\) while retaining rainbow \(C_7\), but their forced
missing-star reserve already has size at least \(D_B=g\).  Thus they
close in Branch A and do not realize hard reserve failure.  Branch A,

other BCM branches remain open.
