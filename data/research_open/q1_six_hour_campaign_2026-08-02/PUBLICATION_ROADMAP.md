# Publication roadmap after the six-hour campaign

Date: 2026-08-02

Status: **RIGOROUS MANUSCRIPT COMPONENTS, NOT YET A SUBMISSION-READY PAPER**

No journal quartile can be guaranteed by a technical package alone.  The
ranking below measures mathematical self-containment and audit maturity,
not an editorial prediction.  All four public problems remain open.

## Priority 1: Erdős #809 maximum-witness degree spread

### Existing paper core

1. Maximum-witness B-opposite setup and four disjoint missing-pair charges.
2. One-leaf reduction, centre-degree concavity, and exact even/odd
   factorization.
3. Parity-sharp square-root degree-spread theorem.
4. Infinite cyclic equality constructions.
5. Eight-template proof of `L_4(2)` for `g>=5`.
6. Exact repeated-colour stress test and proof that it is reserve-paid.
7. Independent reconstruction with graph, path, colour, and arithmetic
   certificates.

### Why it is strongest now

This package has a clean extremal statement, sharp constants, equality
examples, and a transparent proof independent of the longer colour-budget
normal form.  It can stand as the central theorem of a specialized paper
even before #809 is closed, provided the relationship to known
degree-spread and rainbow-cycle literature is established externally.

### Required promotion step

Prove one theorem coupling the sharp degree profile to hard colour/reserve
provenance, or prove a structural stability theorem showing that every
near-sharp graph is forced into the reserve-paid regime.  Also add a
complete literature comparison and polish the construction into standard
graph-theoretic notation.

## Priority 2: Erdős #776 adaptive Macaulay bridges

### Existing paper core

1. Exact one-promotion six-chamber normal form.
2. Five uniform positive chamber theorems.
3. Actual dyadic infinite counterfamily in the sixth chamber.
4. First rank-five failure and all-parameter negative formula.
5. Exact nonstable boundary analysis and stable rank-six recovery.
6. Independent greedy-Macaulay reconstruction and convolution-tail
   certificates.

### Why it is publishable-stage

The counterfamily corrects a natural but false fixed-rank strategy on the
original lattice, rather than in a relaxation, and the immediate adaptive
recovery explains the right replacement mechanism.  This is a substantive
negative-plus-repair result.  Its scope must remain explicit: another
inherited family already rules out a uniform rank-six theorem globally,
and none of these families refutes #776.

### Required promotion step

Extend the adaptive mechanism over the complementary no-borrow lattice and
positive-side cap transitions, or formulate a broader theorem that
classifies precisely when fixed rank fails and how the minimal seed rank
grows.  Then connect that classification to the rank-42 capacity target.

## Priority 3: OPG-1757 base-four Newton coefficients

### Existing paper core

1. Universal second-active positivity in both parities.
2. Exact third-active reduction to two transports.
3. Bulk-range low-column and stable reverse-top-band theorems.
4. Eventual logarithmic-boundary positivity.
5. Explicit obstruction to every fixed-depth termwise-positive merge.
6. Multiple independent symbolic and finite certificates.

### Why it is publishable-stage

The package combines exact coefficient algebra, universal positivity, a
quantitative asymptotic frontier, and a proof-method obstruction.  It has
a coherent internal narrative even though it remains one coefficient
sector of the host problem.

### Required promotion step

Close the remaining `31 <= d < 241 log(s)` layer with an effective or
uniform argument.  The resulting complete third-active theorem would make
the coefficient paper substantially stronger.  A separate transfer is
still needed before claiming arbitrary-host OPG-1757.

## Priority 4: Erdős #1083 signed-switch rigidity

### Existing paper core

1. Cyclotomic positive-multiple ratio bound with signed regularizers.
2. Finite-quotient shadow theorem and sharp aperiodic escape.
3. Independent-`Phi_6` fibre rigidity.
4. Binary-box Hamming diameter theorem and exact exponent separation.
5. Common-`X`, `X -> 3X` obstruction.
6. Independent CRT, torsion, mask, and word certificates.

### Why it is less self-contained today

The algebra is strong, but the bridge from arbitrary exact-block geometry
to the audited models is not yet present.  Without that bridge, this is
best viewed as a toolkit paper or two technical sections rather than a
resolution-oriented manuscript.

### Required promotion step

Obtain a structural dichotomy for arbitrary aperiodic centres or signed
residual divisors, and then prove stability of exact-block extraction from
the original near-extremal configuration.  The common-`X` scalar-copy
interface must be handled explicitly.

## Editorial work common to every route

- Check current literature and priority before choosing a headline theorem.
- Replace campaign notation by a minimal self-contained setup.
- Separate human proofs from executable audit appendices.
- State every computational range and explain why it is a guard rather than
  the proof of an infinite assertion.
- Add a limitations section preserving the public-problem firewall.
- Obtain a fresh mathematical referee-style read independent of this
  campaign before submission.

The recommended lead manuscript is the #809 parity-sharp theorem.  The
#776 package is the strongest corrective companion project; OPG becomes a
stronger standalone coefficient paper if the logarithmic boundary is
closed; #1083 needs one additional structural bridge before it should be
positioned as a full paper rather than a collection of rigorous tools.
