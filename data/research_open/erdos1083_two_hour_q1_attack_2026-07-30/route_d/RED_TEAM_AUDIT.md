## Checks

1. **Sign in the distance formula.**  With
   \(z=w-w'\), the vertical difference is
   \(z+\rho\sin\phi\), so the cross term is
   \(+2\rho z\sin\phi\).  The verifier recomputes the distance from
   Cartesian coordinate squares.

2. **Sine multiplicity.**  A horizontal line on a circle meets it in
   at most two points, so replacing \(s(C)\) by the number of sine
   values loses at most two.

3. **Global tangent set.**  For fixed signed \(A\), each retained
   target plane fixes \(y=A\tan(\beta-\alpha)\).  Squaring can merge
   values and therefore only decreases \(R\); \(R\le M\) is safe.

4. **Different circles give different rows.**  In one fixed
   \((A,\rho)\) fibre, different merged circles have different centre
   heights.  Their differences from the chosen centre form \(Z\).

5. **Energy monotonicity.**
   \(T_z\subset T_\ast\) implies
   \(E^+(T_z,\lambda X)\le E^+(T_\ast,\lambda X)\), since every
   solution counted on the left is also counted on the right.

6. **Block use of the multi-dilate theorem.**  Each block has at most
   \(RS=|T_\ast||X|\) dilations, exactly the theorem's hypothesis.
   When \(H\ge RS\), the sum of full and residual block estimates is
   \(O(RSH)\).

7. **Zero height.**  There is at most one \(z=0\).  Its crude energy
   \(O(RS^2)\) is absorbed by \(O(RSH)\) because \(H\ge RS\ge S\).

8. **Endpoint arithmetic.**  The exact verifier checks collision
   exponent \(187/41\), same-height exponent \(161/41\), gap
   \(26/41\), and exact-minimality gap \(88/41-2=6/41\).

9. **Euclidean model.**  The construction uses real square roots only
   for coordinates whose squared values are positive.  Choosing
   \(K>2UH\) proves all target \(y^2\)'s distinct.  It is a true
   configuration in \(\mathbb R^3\).  Adding translated source
   angular sets makes every target row produce a genuine rich
   radius-one reverse circle.

10. **Model limitation.**  The same distinctness proof shows the
    model uses \(UH\), not \(M\), axial target planes.  It cannot be
    cited as a realization of the Route B endpoint.

11. **Exact versus stable inverse statement.**  The equality
    classification in the real sumset inequality proves Theorem 3.
    Nothing here asserts that \(O(U)\)-sized row sumsets are close
    enough to equality; that would be false without quantitative
    hypotheses adapted to \(U/S\to\infty\).

12. **Publication boundary.**  The cross-height localization is a
    useful new node in the proof tree, but without a power-saving
    upper bound for (21) it is not a proof of Erdős #1083 and not a
    standalone \(1\)-quartile result.

13. **Live \(2/9\) endpoint.**  The updated exact ledger gives
    collision lower exponent \(40/9\), same-height upper exponent
    \(35/9\), and exact-minimality gap \(1/9\).  A hypothetical bound
    \(E_{\rm cross}\ll t^{-\delta}S^2UH\) needs
    \(\delta>1/18\), because \(UH\) itself has \(1/18\) slack below
    the global distance budget.
