## Erdős #809

### What survived audit

The four-bridge two-clique construction has the advertised density, minimum
degree, diameter-three witness, and robust exact-four-path property.  Its
paired colouring is rainbow on every \(C_7\): a cycle crossing the cut four
times has length at least eight, while a two-crossing cycle containing a
paired generic edge in both cliques has two internal paths of length at least
three.

Consequently the raw frozen-\(A\) defect target is genuinely false.  The
second construction is stronger: even the budgeted inequality is false when
quantified over every admissible \(A\).  The numerical witness
\((p,q)=(120,80)\) was independently rerun and gives
\[
D_A=2070>1887.4404\ldots
  =|E_{\rm good}(A)|-\Phi(n,e).
\]

The quantifier firewall is essential.  BCM Lemma 3.1 is existential, and its
maximum-degree branch chooses \(N[v]\).  On the same extremal family that
branch gives \(A_*=P\cup\{q_1\}\) and \(D_{A_*}=0\).  Thus the no-go theorem
kills universal-\(A\) charging, not a canonical-witness strategy.

The dense-core closure proof was checked directly.  Deleting the
high-missing-degree vertices leaves an asymptotically complete core satisfying
\(2\delta(H)-|H|\ge5\).  The two explicit path constructions then put every
two core edges on a common \(C_7\).
It follows that all core edges have distinct colours.  This is a valid
unbounded conditional theorem, but the canonical-witness stability
dichotomy needed to apply it globally remains open.

### First unproved inference

For the witness generated by the actual BCM branches, derive either an
aligned asymptotically complete core or a different family of
\((T(s)-o(1))n^2\) pairwise \(C_7\)-compatible good edges.

## Erdős #776

### What survived audit

The rank-18 zero-slack implication was independently reconstructed:
\[
D_{18}\le P_{18}
\Longrightarrow
D_{17}\le P_{17}+V
\Longrightarrow
D_{16}\le P_{16}+V+\operatorname{KK}_{15}(V)
\le P_{16}+16V.
\]
The residual separation at \(V=288\), the monotonicity of
\(\binom{V-13}{r}/V\), and every fixed-depth coefficient through rank eight
were rerun with exact integers.  The conditional theorem itself does not
use a finite extrapolation.

The one-binomial complement is a useful coordinate, but its recurrence must
not be implemented as the forward defect recurrence.  The exact rule is
\[
C_{r-1}=\operatorname{KK}_r(C_r+V),
\]
not \(V+\operatorname{KK}_r(C_r)\).  Confusing the two produces a false
endpoint and is now an explicit implementation firewall.

Exact recomputation at
\[
V=70,100,175,288,379,1000
\]
gives the stronger pattern
\[
C_2=T(V)-1,
\]
equivalently a reverse endpoint \(V+1\).  This is falsifier/discovery
evidence only.  It is not yet an all-\(V\) theorem.

### First unproved inference

Prove \(D_{18}\le P_{18}\) for every \(V\ge288\), equivalently prove the
single-binomial complement endpoint.  A proof must control all
recanonicalizations; reproducing the observed \(C_2=T-1\) at selected
parameters is not enough.

## OPG-1757

### What survived audit

The high-degree determinant endpoint, the exact normalization, and Newton
inversion give
\[
[\beta^{4s-10}]B_n
=4s^{2s-8}n!
\left({2s-5\brace n}-{2s-6\brace n}\right).
\]
The Stirling recurrence makes its sign positive for
\(2\le n\le2s-5\).  Combining
\(\min\deg_\beta B_n\ge2n\) with
\(\max\deg_\beta B_n\le4s-10\) gives the exact cutoff and makes the deepest
layer a monomial.

The binary-forest and one-ternary-hyperforest description of the next layer
has the correct ordered-page factorial and overlap exclusion.  Its component
sums and closed forms agree with the independent verifier.  The proof is an
unbounded complete-split pooled theorem; it is not an arbitrary-host OPG
theorem.

### First unproved inference

Close all five coefficients of \(B_{2s-7}\), including the two-ternary,
one-quaternary, and one-overlap species, or isolate the first species whose
sign does not admit a uniform positive formula.  The component table should
also be exported with a symbolic algebra certificate before publication.
