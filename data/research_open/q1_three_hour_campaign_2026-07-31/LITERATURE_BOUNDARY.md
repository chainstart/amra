# Literature and priority boundary

Date checked: 2026-07-31

## Search protocol

The search used the `nature-academic-search` multi-source workflow:
T1 primary records first (arXiv and publisher/problem pages), followed by an
OpenAlex discovery pass and ordinary web discovery only to look for newer
neighbours. Records were deduplicated by arXiv identifier/DOI and title.
The OpenAlex keyword pass was noisy for these problem numbers, so no claim
below relies on it.

## Erdős #809

The controlling primary comparison remains:

- Matija Bucić, Kaizhe Chen and Jie Ma, *On a maximal anti-Ramsey
  conjecture of Burr, Erdős, Graham, and Sós*, arXiv:2603.18952,
  19 March 2026.

Its stated theorem proves the full asymptotic formula for
\(C_{2k+1}\) when \(k\ge4\). It explicitly does not include \(k=3\),
namely \(C_7\). Searches through 31 July 2026 found later maximal
anti-Ramsey work on \(P_4\), but no primary-source paper claiming the
missing \(C_7\) case.

Therefore a correct closure of the fixed-\(s\) Case-1 branch would still
cross a live public boundary. The present campaign's four-bridge family
does not refute that conjecture; it attains its colour target and only
refutes an overstrong intermediate linkage-defect estimate.

## Erdős #776

The controlling recent comparison remains:

- Yixin He and Quanyu Tang, *An Erdős--Trotter problem on antichains with
  multiplicity \(r\) on each occurring level*, arXiv:2602.09803,
  10 February 2026.

They prove
\[
2r+2\le n_0(r)
\le2r+2\log_2r+O(\log_2\log_2r).
\]
No primary source found in the 31 July search claims
\(n_0(r)\le2r+5\) or the sharper colex endpoint pursued here.
Consequently an all-\(V\) proof of the remaining zero-slack premise
\(D_{18}\le P_{18}\), combined with the proved gate, would be a substantial
improvement rather than a restatement of that preprint.

## OPG-1757

The primary historical and recent neighbours are:

- G. R. Grimmett and S. N. Winkler, *Negative association in uniform
  forests and connected graphs*, arXiv:math/0302185;
- Dudley Stark, *The Edge Correlation of Random Forests*, Annals of
  Combinatorics 15 (2011), 529--539, DOI
  10.1007/s00026-011-0104-7;
- Pengfei Tang and Zibo Zhang, *Pairwise Negative Correlation for Uniform
  Spanning Subgraphs of the Complete Graph*, arXiv:2603.10738;
- Hao Fang and Biao Ma, *Gårding Polynomials*, arXiv:2604.27755;
- Hao Fang and Biao Ma, *Ideal Gårding Polynomials*, arXiv:2607.16832.

Grimmett--Winkler formulate the uniform-forest negative-correlation
problem and report finite graph verification. Stark proves the full
uniform-forest inequality for \(K_n\) when \(n\) is sufficiently large.
Tang--Zhang prove p-NC on \(K_n\) for forests with any fixed number \(k\)
of components when \(n\) is sufficiently large (and record the
all-admissible-\(n\) result for \(k=2,3,4\)).  Their primary paper contains
no complete-split specialization and no pooled Newton-layer theorem.
Fang--Ma prove broad structural
Gårding/Rayleigh consequences for specified matroid classes and operations;
the newer ideal-Gårding preprint is structural and does not state a forest
or graphic-family specialization covering the present model.
Nothing in the primary statements located here supplies the complete-split
all-depth pooled coefficient theorem or an arbitrary-host solution of
OPG-1757.

The safe novelty boundary for this campaign is therefore an exact
complete-split top-face/deep-layer theorem, not a claim of arbitrary-host
negative correlation. A full priority audit before submission would still
need MathSciNet/zbMATH/Scopus coverage and direct comparison of the detailed
Fang--Ma closure classes.
