### OPG-1757

The strongest result is unconditional: every normalized endpoint
\(Q_{h,e,c}\) is a polynomial of exact degree \(2c+2e-2\); hence every
fixed-deficit denominator cancels. For every fixed \(q\ge0\) and
\(0\le r\le2q\),

\[
\deg R_{q,r}=2q+r,\qquad
[s^{2q+r}]R_{q,r}=\frac4{q!}[z^r](1+2z+2z^2)^q>0.
\]

Therefore all \(2q+1\) coefficients in the natural support of the layer
are positive for sufficiently large \(s\). Two independent audits passed
the all-\(q\) proof. Their initial objection to the universal second
symbol was then closed by a new filtered-ring lemma proving that Laurent
loss \(k\) has joint profile degree at most \(2k\); an independent
line-by-line audit and a new three-marking symbolic firewall passed.
The growing-deficit window remains conditional on one explicit uniform
height lemma.

Writing \(A(z)=1+2z+2z^2\), the newly certified second symbol is
\[
\sum_{r=0}^{2q}[s^{2q-1}]C_{q,r}(s)z^r
=\frac4{q!}A(z)^{q-2}qP_q(z),
\]
with the explicit quartic \(P_q\) recorded in the OPG report.
