## Round 3: full-system Fejer route

### Continuous route: exact obstruction

The reconstruction-safe continuous half-width is `b_i=d_i/2`.  The tempting
larger width `(d_i+1)/2` is invalid: positive located intervals can intersect
in `(N,N+1)` without containing a common integer time.

For the safe width, the positive spectral identity has local mass two when
`d_i=1`, equivalently when `k+1` is prime.  It therefore forces `S>=2` and
kills the strict continuous full-system majorant for infinitely many
relevant parameter shapes.  Increasing the constant does not remove this
local obstruction; the guarded `k=30` values stabilize above two through
`C=6`.

### Exact discrete repair

For `r_i+s_i=d_i+1`, let `tau_i` be the convolution of uniform probabilities
on `[0,r_i-1]` and `[0,s_i-1]`.  Its positive support is exactly
`[0,d_i-1]`.  The integer triangular time count has exact Fourier expansion

```text
N(c) = P^(-1) sum_a W_h(A(a)/P) e_P(A(a)c) product_i T_i(a_i).
```

The zero word contributes `h/P`.  Hence

```text
sum_(a != 0) W_h(A(a)/P) product_i |T_i(a_i)| < h
```

is a rigorous all-center sufficient condition with no continuous
reconstruction step.

If `p_0=k+1`, choosing the physical window `h=p_0 H` and using

```text
D_(p_0 H)(x)=D_(p_0)(x)D_H(p_0 x)
```

plus finite Parseval sums the full `p_0` frequency fibre exactly.  The zero
fibre is removed exactly, and the remaining problem has the same form with
one specific dilation `gamma=p_0` and only polynomial physical cost.

### Narrowest open theorem

AM-GM converts the absolute local Fourier product to nonnegative compact
triangles.  With their product mass `L_G`, the exact positive dual is

```text
Q_(h,gamma) = sum_(|ell|<h) (1-|ell|/h)
              product_i phi_i(gamma ell).
```

The factor-two inequality `Q_(h,gamma)<2h/L_G` is sufficient.  A more
usable sufficient discrepancy statement is

```text
Q_(h,gamma) <= h/L_G + K^m
```

for one absolute `K`, uniformly for `gamma=1` and the one required
`gamma=p_0`.  Choosing the window base `C>2K` would prove `n_k=exp(o(k))`.

Several exact boundaries are now known:

- every fixed base `C<2` is impossible for this positive majorant, even
  after multiplying by `k^B`;
- for `gamma=1`, the complete prefix `|ell|<k` costs only `O(log k)`:
  the part below `k/2` decays geometrically and the annulus from `k/2` to
  `k` is identically zero;
- the remaining undilated tail starts at `|ell|>=k`; the `gamma=p_0` case
  does not inherit this prefix theorem;
- support is exactly `2|N-qp|<=p-k`, with quotient intervals
  `(2N+k)/(2q+1)<=p<=(2N-k)/(2q-1)` and adjacent gap
  `2k/(2q+1)`.  At high frequency the gap has scale `k^2/N`; once `N`
  exceeds a fixed constant multiple of `k^2`, every active gap is shorter
  than one.  Ordinary prime-gap or fixed-quotient counting therefore does
  not control the weighted product.

### Scoped methods eliminated

The following ledgers provably retain an `exp(Theta(k))` loss or an
inadmissible main-factor loss:

- global `L1`, interval Cauchy/global `L2`, and separated Holder moments;
- support-only larger sieve;
- proper-subset complete-period conditioning;
- generic Fejer averaging plus complete-period mean;
- energy thresholding followed by trivial cardinality;
- coordinatewise or fixed-quotient prime counts that discard the joint
  triangular distance product.

These are method no-goes, not lower bounds on the true coupled sum.
