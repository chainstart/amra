# Target selection: residue-aware rounding

Let `H_N` be the bad-support hypergraph for distinct Finset products on
`{2,...,N}`.  The finite target remains

\[
\tau(H_N)=o(N).                                      \tag{T1}
\]

The natural-density target requires one compatible infinite decision rule,
not a sequence of unrelated solutions to (T1).

## Audited immutable inputs

1. The log-defect weight
   `w_N(n)=log(N/n)/log N` covers every bad support and has total
   `(1+o(1))N/log N`.
2. IR.1 embeds minimal bad circuits in every moving lower-power tail whose
   deletion cost is `o(N)`.  Hence hard/nested thresholds and affordable
   unaltered independent proportional samples are closed negative routes.

Neither predecessor campaign may be modified.

## Required comparison

The portfolio must keep separate:

* dependent arithmetic alteration, where failure events are compressed by
  shared valuation structure;
* deterministic largest-prime ownership, where the lower-prime cofactor
  residue is part of the owner state;
* recursive residue-token potentials, whose decisions could stabilize over
  growing prime cylinders.

A route is circular if its state contains the entire original relation but
has no proved smaller potential, lower scale, or bounded amortized load.
Finite cutoffs and generic set-cover bounds are falsification tools only.
