# Representation search: the all-parameter rank-8 entry

The search keeps the public threshold and the local rank-eight interface
separate.  Ten representations in six families were tested for information
that is absent from the scalar defect recurrence.

## The two representations that expose a plausible new invariant

1. **Separated canonical prefix/suffix.**  If two canonical words have the
   same high prefix, the shadow of that prefix cancels exactly.  Only the
   lower suffix should be charged.  This is the missing overlap credit in both
   the independent-tax and blind adjacent couplings.
2. **Dyadic carry blocks.**  The top upper index in the rank-six word of
   `W_6` can change only on a binomial carry wall.  Charging such walls to
   parameter doublings would turn a finite empirical logarithmic bound into
   an all-parameter theorem.

The adjacent-margin representation packages the first route as

\[
 M(V)={V-13\choose6}-W_6(V),\qquad
 M(V+1)-M(V)={V-13\choose5}-[W_6(V+1)-W_6(V)].
\]

Thus one exact all-`V` suffix-loss inequality and the independently checked
base `M(40)=260272` would close the local rank-eight interface.

## Representations retained only for falsification or dependency control

Colex missing sets and squarefree Hilbert functions are legitimate semantic
models, but they add nothing unless they preserve a preimage or multiplication
map that the scalar recurrence has discarded.  Reverse capacity must permit
the exact `V=288` temporary crossing at rank 16.  Normalized scalar capacity
may suggest a true inequality but does not explain it.  Finally, the public
threshold ledger prevents a proof of the local interface from being reported
as an exact value of `n_0(r)`.

The twelve mechanism statements are deliberately stronger and individually
falsifiable.  Finite scans are used only as counterexample searches; the two
plausible statements remain candidates until an all-parameter carry argument
is supplied.
