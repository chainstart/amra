# Target selection

Let \(H_N\) be the bad-relation hypergraph for the distinct-Finset form of
Erdős 786.  Its vertices are \(2,\ldots,N\), and an edge is the support of
two disjoint finite sets of distinct integers with equal products and
different cardinalities.

The original finite and infinite density-one questions remain the public
targets.  This round has one deliberately narrower promotion contract.  It
must strengthen the predecessor equal-defect projective-plane cluster from a
logarithmic blocking number to a stretched-exponential blocking number.
Specifically, for every sufficiently large integer \(N\), it seeks a connected
subfamily \(\mathcal F_N\subseteq H_N\) such that

\[
  \mathcal F_N\subset (N/64,N],\qquad
  \nu(\mathcal F_N)=1,\qquad
  \tau(\mathcal F_N)\ge 2^{\sqrt{\log_2 N}/200},
\]

every two supports meet in exactly one integer, every support is minimal in
the cancelled \(\{-1,0,1\}\)-coefficient sense, and all normalized
cardinality defects are equal.  The proof must include the reduction from an
arbitrary \(N\) to a power of two and may not extrapolate from finite
projective planes checked by computation.

This is a standalone quantitative theorem about an arithmetic subhypergraph.
It is not an upper bound for \(\tau(H_N)\), does not prove
\(\tau(H_N)=o(N)\), and does not produce an infinite density-one set.
