# K5-e high-triangle marked-edge stabilizer attack

Date: 2026-08-03

Status: **exact three-variable routing and one component theorem; full
stabilizer component open; no promotion**

## 1. Host, marked orbit, and scope

Let the host be `K5` with the low--low edge `34` deleted.  Vertices
`0,1,2` have degree four and form the high-vertex triangle; vertices `3,4`
have degree three.  Fix the high--high marked edge `01`.

The graph automorphisms preserving `01` setwise form a group of order four,
generated by `0<->1` and `3<->4`.  Its orbits on the eight unmarked edges
are exactly

\[
 a:\{02,12\},\qquad
 b:\{03,04,13,14\},\qquad
 c:\{23,24\},
\]

of sizes `2,4,2`.

This note studies the complete **three-variable stabilizer specialization**.
The fixed marked graph still has eight independent unmarked edge variables.
Equalizing them on the three orbits loses five directions.  Therefore a
proof below is not a proof for the eight-variable host and not a proof of
global `G201`.  Conversely, a valid counterexample inside this slice would
also be a counterexample to the eight-variable statement, but none is found.

The audited `K3,3` certificate is not modified or used as a computational
dependency.

## 2. Independent complement-of-forest enumeration

For each forest `I` of the marked-edge deletion, give its complement the
monomial

\[
 a^{|I^c\cap O_a|}b^{|I^c\cap O_b|}c^{|I^c\cap O_c|}.
\]

Summing over every forest gives `P=C_{M\setminus01}`.  Summing only over
forests in which `0` and `1` are connected gives
`xi=C_{M\setminus01}-C_{M/01}`.  This second criterion follows because
exactly those forests acquire a cycle after adding the marked edge.

Independent enumeration gives 134 forests, 70 of them connecting the
marked endpoints, and

\[
 P=bF,\qquad \xi=bG.
\]

The residual polynomials are

\[
\begin{aligned}
F={}&a^2b^3c^2+2a^2b^3c+a^2b^3
 +4a^2b^2c^2+8a^2b^2c+4a^2b^2\\
&+6a^2bc^2+12a^2bc+4a^2b+4a^2c^2+8a^2c\\
&+2ab^3c^2+4ab^3c+2ab^3
 +8ab^2c^2+12ab^2c+4ab^2\\
&+12abc^2+12abc+8ac^2
 +b^3c^2+2b^3c+b^3+4b^2c^2+4b^2c+4bc^2,
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
G={}&2a^2bc^2+4a^2bc+4a^2b+4a^2c^2+8a^2c\\
&+4ab^2c+4ab^2+4abc^2+12abc+8ac^2\\
&+b^3c^2+2b^3c+b^3+4b^2c^2+4b^2c+4bc^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

At the positive anchor `(1,1,1)`, they give `P=134` and `xi=70`.
Since `P=bF`, the sign of `b` cannot change inside the distinguished
component; hence `b>0` there and domination reduces to `G>0` on the
distinguished component of `F>0`.

## 3. Zero-locus elimination

Eliminating `a` from the full `P,xi` gives the exact resultant

\[
 \operatorname{Res}_a(P,\xi)=b^8L^6H,
\]

where

\[
 L=bc+b+2c,
\]

\[
 H=L^2+8c
  =b^2c^2+2b^2c+b^2+4bc^2+4bc+4c^2+8c.
\]

In shifted variables `y=b+1,z=c+1`, these become

\[
 L=z(y+1)-2,
\]

\[
 H=[z(y+1)-2]^2+8(z-1)
  =z^2(y+1)^2-4yz+4z-4.
\]

The pseudo-remainder in `a` is

\[
 \operatorname{prem}_a(F,G)=-b^2L^2T,
\]

with

\[
\begin{aligned}
T={}&4abc+4ab+4ac+8a+b^2c^2+2b^2c+b^2\\
   &+4bc^2+8bc+4b+4c^2+8c.
\end{aligned}
\]

The leading coefficient of `G` as a quadratic in `a` is `2D`, where

\[
 D=bc^2+2bc+2b+2c^2+4c.
\]

Consequently, away from `D L=0`, the exact identity on `G=0` is

\[
 \boxed{2D F=-b^2L^2T.}
\]

Thus a zero of `G` lying inside `F>0` must have `DT<0`.  Proving that the
distinguished component never meets this sign cell, with separate handling
of `D=0` and `L=0`, is the remaining three-variable component-wall problem.
The resultant alone does not decide it.

## 4. The anchor component forces neither z>=1 nor H>=0

Set `a=b=1` and move `c` from `1` to `-1/10`.  Along this entire segment,

\[
 P=2(27c^2+32c+8).
\]

Its derivative is positive on `[-1/10,1]`, and the left-end value is
`507/50>0`.  The segment is therefore an explicit path in the full
three-variable distinguished component.  At its endpoint,

\[
 z=\frac9{10}<1,qquad H=-\frac{31}{100}<0,
\]

while

\[
 P=\frac{507}{50},\qquad \xi=\frac{587}{100}>0.
\]

Hence neither proposed shortcut `z>=1` nor `H>=0` holds on the anchor
component.  The endpoint is not a certificate against domination.

## 5. Complete component proof on b=1

Although the full three-variable problem remains open, the whole `b=1`
slice can be settled, not merely sampled.  Put

\[
\begin{aligned}
u={}&ac+\left(1-\frac{\sqrt{10}}5\right)(a+c)
       +\frac13-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{15},\\
v={}&ac+\left(1+\frac{\sqrt{10}}5\right)(a+c)
       +\frac13+\frac{\sqrt{10}}{15}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Exact factorization gives

\[
 F(a,1,c)=15uv.
\]

Both `u` and `v` are positive at `(a,c)=(1,1)`.  Along any path in the
distinguished component of `F(a,1,c)>0`, neither factor can vanish, so that
component is contained in the chamber `u>0,v>0`.

Solving for `a+c` and `ac` in terms of `u,v` and substituting into `G` gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
4G={}&u^2+22uv+\left(8+\frac{8\sqrt{10}}3\right)u\\
 &+v^2+\left(8-\frac{8\sqrt{10}}3\right)v+\frac49.
\end{aligned}
\]

The first three terms are positive for `u,v>0`.  The remaining quadratic in
`v` has positive leading coefficient and discriminant

\[
 -\frac{16}{3}(8\sqrt{10}-25)<0,
\]

because `sqrt(10)>25/8`.  Therefore

\[
 \boxed{G>0}
\]

throughout the complete distinguished component of the `b=1` slice.

## 6. A competing zero-locus component, not an anchor counterexample

The exact line

\[
 a=-\frac b2,\qquad c=0,qquad b>0
\]

lies inside `F>0` and on `G=0`, since

\[
 F=\frac{b^5}{4},\qquad G=0.
\]

At the nearby rational point

\[
 (a,b,c)=\left(-\frac12,1,-\frac1{100}\right)
\]

one has

\[
 F=\frac{10191}{40000}>0,qquad
 G=-\frac{191}{20000}<0.
\]

This is an exact negative zero-locus-side model.  It is **not** a domination
counterexample: on `b=1` both wall factors satisfy `u<0,v<0`, whereas the
distinguished component has `u>0,v>0`.  It supplies a useful competing
component against which any three-variable component proof must firewall.
In particular it explains why a rational point satisfying only `P>0` and
`xi<0` is insufficient.

## 7. Disposition

- Stabilizer and orbit sizes: **proved**, order four and `2,4,2`.
- Complement-of-forest polynomials: **proved by independent enumeration**.
- Shortcuts `z>=1` and `H>=0`: **strictly refuted on the anchor component**.
- Complete `b=1` component: **proved safe**, `xi>0`.
- A separate `F>0`, `G<0` component: **exhibited exactly**, but proved not
  distinguished on `b=1`.
- Full three-variable stabilizer component: **open**; neither domination nor
  a counterexample is established.
- Eight-independent-variable `K5-e` host: **not tested by this proof**.
- Global `G201`: **unchanged**.
