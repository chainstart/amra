# Primary-source context for survivor M008

Checked: 2026-08-02.

The exact `q -> 0` boundary identity in this campaign is a representation
bridge, not a new solution strategy by itself.

- Grimmett and Winkler, *Negative association in uniform forests and connected
  graphs* (2004 preprint version: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0302185), place the
  uniform-forest conjecture inside the broader random-cluster correlation
  conjecture and report finite verification, not an arbitrary-host proof.
- Nguyen and Pylyavskyy, *Correlations in random cluster model at q=1*
  (https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09520), state that edge negative correlation for
  `0 <= q <= 1` remains conjectural and obtain a combinatorial formula at the
  endpoint `q=1`.
- Huang, *On Negative Correlation of Arboreal Gas on Some Graphs*
  (https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00965), treats specific graph families and also
  describes general arboreal-gas negative correlation as open.
- Tang and Zhang, *Pairwise Negative Correlation for Uniform Spanning
  Subgraphs of the Complete Graph* (https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10738), prove
  results for selected uniform families on sufficiently large complete graphs;
  this does not supply the weighted arbitrary-host finite-`q` premise required
  by M008.

Accordingly, replacing OPG-1757 by finite-`q` random-cluster negative
dependence would replace the target with a broader open premise.  M008 may
remain a representation for importing a genuinely new finite-`q` certificate,
but the boundary lemma alone receives no promotion credit.
