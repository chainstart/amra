# K5-e cross-edge singleton-orbit component theorem

## Scope

Let the host be `K5` with edge `34` deleted and mark the cross edge `03`.
The stabilizer of `03` has order two, generated by `1<->2`.  Its complete
orbit ledger on the eight unmarked edges is

```text
a={01,02}, b={04}, c={12}, d={13,23}, e={14,24},
```

with sizes `2,1,1,2,2`.  Thus the complete stabilizer-fixed space has five
variables and orbit equalization erases three transverse edge directions.

This theorem treats only the natural singleton-orbit slice `a=d=e=1`, with
`b,c` free.  It does not prove the five-variable stabilizer statement, the
eight-independent-variable marked host, G201, or OPG-1757.

## Exact forest reconstruction

Enumerating all subsets of the eight-edge deletion graph gives 128 forests.
Exactly 58 connect the marked endpoints 0 and 3.  Weighting complements by
their orbit activities reconstructs `P=C_(M\03)` from all forests and
`xi=C_(M\03)-C_(M/03)` from the endpoint-connected subfamily.  The all-one
values are therefore `P=128` and `xi=58`.

On `a=d=e=1`, exact collection gives

```text
P  = 54bc+27b+32c+15,
xi = 2(11bc+6b+8c+4).
```

## Complete distinguished component

Use the invertible affine coordinates

```text
x=54b+32,  y=c+1/2.
```

Then `P=xy-1`.  The set `{P>0}` has exactly two components: `x,y>0,
xy>1` and `x,y<0,xy>1`.  The positive anchor `(b,c)=(1,1)` maps to
`(x,y)=(86,3/2)`, so its complete distinguished component is exactly

```text
D={x>0,y>0,xy>1}.
```

The domination polynomial becomes

```text
54xi=22xy+x+160y-32.
```

On `D`, `y>1/x`, hence

```text
54xi > 22+x+160/x-32
     = x+160/x-10
     = (x-5)^2/x+135/x
     > 0.
```

Therefore

```text
xi>0
```

on the complete distinguished component of this two-variable slice.

As an algebraic cross-check,

```text
Res_c(P,xi)=2(27b^2+27b+8),
```

whose quadratic discriminant is `-135`.  The component proof above is
stronger than the bare resultant statement because it identifies the anchor
component and supplies a direct sign bound.

## Disposition

- Cross-edge stabilizer and five orbit variables: proved exactly.
- Forest `P` and endpoint-connected `xi`: reconstructed exactly.
- Ten natural unit two-variable slices: scanned for rational straight-anchor
  counterexamples; none was found in the recorded finite grid.
- Complete `(b,c)` distinguished component: proved safe.
- Remaining five-variable cross stabilizer component: open.
- Public OPG-1757 statement: unchanged.

Reproduction uses a 3 GiB virtual-memory limit and 180-second timeout.  Lean
was not needed.
